# Write a page

Write the page in Markdown, with the site's components in the sidebar: drag one in, or type `/`. The preview sits beside it, and **Ctrl+.** opens a component's form when you'd rather fill one in. Download the page with its images and files as one bundle; nothing leaves your computer until you upload it, and **Add to site** shows you how.
